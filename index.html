How it works

Three steps. Zero spreadsheets.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Step 1

Add your people

Type them in, paste a list from your group chat, or pick from your contacts.

✉️Step 2

They write letters to Santa

Sizes, three wishes and gift hints, in English or Spanish. Each letter lands on your page automatically.

🪅Step 3

Draw names, send piñatas

One tap draws names (partners can't get each other). Everyone breaks a piñata to see who they're shopping for.
