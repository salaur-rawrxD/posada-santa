Privacy & terms

Last updated November 7, 2026 · Santa mailboxes, secret questions and smart wish matching added

The short version

Posada Santa is a free tool for running a family or friends Secret Santa. Only the organizer makes an account (an email and a code, no password). Family members never need one. There are no ads, and we don't sell anything you enter.

What stays on your phone

  • If the organizer doesn't log in, their list of people, phone numbers, couples, and the name draw stay in that phone's browser storage only.
  • Game progress, outfits, and draft letters are saved on the device that's playing.

What we store on our servers

  • Letters to Santa: when someone seals a letter, it's stored in an encoded form so the organizer's page can pick it up. Only the organizer's page, which holds a private key created on their phone, can read a group's letters. Letters are deleted automatically 120 days after they're sent.
  • Game scores: the player name and score for a group's leaderboard. Anyone with that group's game link can see its board. Scores are deleted after 180 days.
  • Group name: the name of the Secret Santa, so letters reach the right organizer.
  • Organizer accounts: the organizer's email address, and their Secret Santa groups (names, phone numbers they entered, couples, the draw and letters) so they can open them from any device. Only the logged-in organizer can read their groups. Login codes are emailed through our email provider, Resend.
  • Posada Plus purchases: payments are handled by Stripe. We never see card numbers. We keep which group was upgraded, the receipt email and the amount.
  • Tips ("Buy us a tamal"): optional, handled entirely on Stripe's checkout page. Tips go to EVA Systems LLC, the small business that runs Posada Santa, and are not tax-deductible donations.
  • Secret questions and Santa mailboxes: when names are drawn, each person gets a private Santa mailbox, opened only by a secret inside their own piñata link. When a Santa pays $1.99 to ask their match a question, we store the question, the match's name and group, and later the answer. We never store who asked: not their name, email or phone, and the Stripe payment isn't linked to anyone in the group. Questions go straight to the match's mailbox; the organizer only sees how many questions are waiting in the group, never for whom, what they say or who asked. Questions, answers and mailboxes are deleted after 120 days.
  • Alert emails (optional): if someone adds an email in their letter or mailbox, we keep it only to tell them a secret question arrived, sent through Resend. It is never added to the letter, so the organizer never sees it. Deleted after 120 days.

Our database is hosted by Supabase and the site by Vercel. Their servers log ordinary technical data like IP addresses for security.

Visit counts: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies, to count page visits and a few anonymous moments (like "a letter was sent" or "names were drawn"). Before anything is counted, we remove the private part of every link (everything after the #), so piñata codes, wishes, names and mailbox secrets never leave your browser.

Messages

Invites, reminders, and piñata links are sent from your own phone through WhatsApp or text. We never send messages or see your contacts. If you use "Pick from contacts," the names and numbers you select go into your browser only.

Shopping links

When someone opens their match, each wish can have a "Find it" link to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Posada Santa earns from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. The link only contains the wish text; we don't send Amazon any names.

On the gift ideas page, the three wishes plus a few letter answers (sizes, style, colors, what they love; never names) are sent to Anthropic's Claude to turn each wish into a better Amazon search. Results are cached for up to 60 days so the same wish isn't sent twice.

Kids

The game is made to be family-friendly. We don't run ads, profile players, or collect anything from players besides the name they type and their scores. A parent or organizer should set up groups that include children.

Deleting things

Organizers can delete their Secret Santa from the Settings tab, which removes it from their phone and their account. To have an account, letters or scores removed from our servers, email hola@posadaxmas.com.

Terms

Posada Santa is provided as-is, for personal gift exchanges. Please don't use it to collect information from people who haven't agreed to take part. Posada Plus is a one-time payment for one group; if something went wrong with your purchase, email us within 30 days for a refund. We may change or retire features at any time.
